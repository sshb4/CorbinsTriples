Corbin Triples SMS terms

Updated September 15, 2026

Corbin Triples is an SMS alert program operated by Sasha Bates. These terms govern the program and your subscription. It is an independent fan project, not affiliated with Corbin Carroll, the Arizona Diamondbacks, or Major League Baseball.

Before alerts launch, website signup saves your phone number and consent request without sending a text. We retain these pre-launch requests for up to 90 days. After launch, we will request confirmation by text; you must reply YES within 15 minutes of that text before receiving triple alerts. Contact ssh.b.designs@gmail.com to cancel a saved request before launch.

What you're signing up for

Enter your US phone number on our homepage, check the box agreeing to automated texts, complete the security check, and submit your signup request. Before launch, this saves your request without sending a text. After launch, reply YES within 15 minutes of receiving your confirmation text to activate your subscription. You agree to receive recurring automated texts about Corbin Carroll's regular-season triples at that number. Frequency varies with his performance. Signup confirmations and service replies may also be sent. Enrollment is limited to US numbers and available capacity.

Cost and cancellation

We do not charge a subscription fee. Your carrier's message and data rates may apply. Consent is not a condition of purchase. Reply STOP to cancel at any time. A final opt-out confirmation may be sent. For help rejoining after opting out, contact ssh.b.designs@gmail.com.

Help and delivery

Reply HELP for assistance. Contact Sasha Bates at ssh.b.designs@gmail.com.

Alerts depend on MLB data and carrier delivery and may be delayed, missed, or affected by scoring corrections. We do not guarantee instant delivery or issue an alert for every later scoring revision. Carriers are not liable for delayed or undelivered messages. We may pause or discontinue the service.
