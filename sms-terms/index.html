Corbin Triples SMS terms

Updated September 14, 2026

Corbin Triples is an independent fan project, not affiliated with Corbin Carroll, the Arizona Diamondbacks, or Major League Baseball.

What you're signing up for

Enter your US phone number on our homepage, check the box agreeing to automated texts, and request a confirmation text. Alternatively, text TRIPLES to (520) 777-0150. With either method, reply YES within 15 minutes to confirm. You agree to receive recurring automated texts about Corbin Carroll's regular-season triples at that number. Frequency varies with his performance. Signup confirmations and service replies may also be sent. Enrollment is limited to US numbers and available capacity.

Cost and cancellation

We do not charge a subscription fee. Your carrier's message and data rates may apply. Consent is not a condition of purchase. Reply STOP to cancel at any time. A final opt-out confirmation may be sent. To rejoin after opting out, text START to unblock messages, then TRIPLES and YES to enroll again.

Help and delivery

Reply HELP for assistance. Contact: Support details will be published before subscriptions open.

Alerts depend on MLB data and carrier delivery and may be delayed, missed, or affected by scoring corrections. We do not guarantee instant delivery or issue an alert for every later scoring revision. Carriers are not liable for delayed or undelivered messages. We may pause or discontinue the service.
