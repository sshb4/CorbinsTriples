# Corbin’s Triples

He triples. You get a text.

Follow Corbin Carroll’s triples at **[corbinstriples.com](https://corbinstriples.com)**. Check his stats, see where he ranks, and sign up for optional text alerts when he hits another one.

## What you can do

- Check Corbin’s season and career triple totals.
- Compare his numbers on the season and career leaderboards.
- Browse his recent triples and stats by season.
- Subscribe to texts for future regular-season triples.

The stats are open to everyone. No account, phone number, or text subscription is needed to browse.

## Get triple texts

1. Open [Triple alerts](https://corbinstriples.com/#sms-alerts) on the website.
2. Enter your US phone number.
3. Check the box agreeing to receive alerts, complete the security check, and submit.
4. Reply **YES within 15 minutes** of the confirmation text.

You’ll receive a subscription confirmation once you’re enrolled. Alerts cover future regular-season triples after you subscribe; you won’t receive a backlog of past plays. Texts come from **(520) 777-0150**, and delivery can take a few minutes.

Signing up for texts is optional. Use **Continue without texts** to keep browsing without submitting your number.

## Manage your subscription

- **STOP** — unsubscribe from alerts.
- **HELP** — get support information.
- **Rejoin after stopping** — contact [ssh.b.designs@gmail.com](mailto:ssh.b.designs@gmail.com). Replying START or UNSTOP unblocks messages but does not resubscribe you.

Message frequency varies with Corbin’s triples. Message and data rates may apply. US phone numbers only.

If you joined the pre-launch waitlist, saving your number did not activate a subscription. You still need a confirmation text and a YES reply to enroll.
