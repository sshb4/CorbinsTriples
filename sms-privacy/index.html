SMS privacy notice

Updated September 14, 2026

Information used for alerts

When you text us, our messaging provider processes your phone number, message contents, and delivery metadata. Our subscription database stores your number, signup and confirmation times, subscription status, terms version, and message identifiers and delivery results. We use this information to confirm subscriptions, send requested alerts, honor opt-outs, and prevent duplicate messages.

Service providers

Twilio handles text delivery and Cloudflare hosts our alert service and subscription database. Information is shared with service providers only as needed to operate the service. Mobile numbers, SMS opt-in data, and consent are not sold or shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes.

Retention and your choices

Your active subscription remains until you unsubscribe or the service ends. Our routine cleanup removes unconfirmed signup records after seven days, stopped subscription records after 90 days, and individual alert delivery records after 90 days. Provider logs and backups may have separate retention periods. Reply STOP to end alerts. For access or deletion requests, contact the support address that will be published before subscriptions open.

Website analytics

The homepage uses Google Analytics to understand website visits. We do not send SMS subscriber numbers or message contents to Google Analytics.
