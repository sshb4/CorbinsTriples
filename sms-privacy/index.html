SMS privacy notice

Updated September 16, 2026

Corbin Triples is operated by Sasha Bates and sends automated alerts about Corbin Carroll's regular-season triples. Message frequency varies. Message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP to cancel or HELP for help.

Before alerts launch, website signup saves your phone number and consent request without sending a text. We retain these pre-launch requests for up to 90 days. After launch, we will request confirmation by text; you must reply YES within 15 minutes of that text before receiving triple alerts. Contact ssh.b.designs@gmail.com to cancel a saved request before launch.

Information used for alerts

Only when you select the optional SMS checkbox and submit a signup request do we save your phone number and consent. Leaving the checkbox unchecked or choosing “Continue without texts” does not submit your number to our alert service. Our messaging provider processes message contents and delivery metadata. Our subscription database stores your number, signup method, signup and confirmation times, subscription status, terms version, and message identifiers and delivery results. We use this information to confirm subscriptions, send requested alerts, honor opt-outs, and prevent duplicate messages.

The web form uses Cloudflare Turnstile to prevent automated abuse. Cloudflare processes browser and network information for this security check. We store keyed hashes of your IP address and phone number in short-lived rate-limit records, retained for up to 48 hours plus the next cleanup cycle. We retain web signup request records for 90 days. Submitting the form saves your request before launch. Confirmation texts are sent after launch; enrollment only occurs after a YES reply.

Service providers

Twilio handles text delivery and Cloudflare hosts our alert service and subscription database. Information is shared with service providers only as needed to operate the service. Mobile numbers, SMS opt-in data, and consent are not sold or shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes.

Retention and your choices

Your active subscription remains until you unsubscribe or the service ends. Our routine cleanup removes unconfirmed signup records after seven days, stopped subscription records after 90 days, and individual alert delivery records after 90 days. Provider logs and backups may have separate retention periods. Reply STOP to end alerts. For access or deletion requests, contact Sasha Bates at ssh.b.designs@gmail.com.

Website analytics

The homepage uses Google Analytics to understand website visits. We do not send SMS subscriber numbers or message contents to Google Analytics.
